# Playbook: presenter to camera

**Use when** one person talks to camera (course, explainer, founder video).

## Spec

- 1080×1920 or 1920×1080, 30 fps, loudness −14 LUFS
- captions `bold-pop` on vertical, `clean` on horizontal
- 60–120 s typical

## Structure

1. Claim or question in the first 2 s, no logo intro
2. 3 points, one per beat; each point gets its own shot or punch-in
3. Graphics/keywords as lower-thirds, never full-screen text over the face
4. Close with the takeaway, not "thanks for watching"

## Editing

- tight silence removal, filler removal, 30 ms audio fades
- punch-in ≤1.25× on emphasis, reset on each point
- face-centred crop (reframe.py) with the eyes in the upper third
- captions never overlap the mouth

## Gates

hook ≤1.5 s · face inside the crop every second · caption safe zone ·
loudness ±1 LU · no freeze >2 s
