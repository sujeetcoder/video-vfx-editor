# Playbook: wedding / event highlights

**Use when** cutting a ceremony, reception or event into highlights.

## Spec

- 1920×1080, 24 or 30 fps matching the source
- 2.5–6 s shots, cinematic pacing, loudness −16 LUFS
- captions optional; if used, `clean` and sparse (vows only)

## Structure

1. Cold open on a detail or reaction (2–4 s)
2. Chronological spine: arrival → ceremony → couple → party
3. Keep the vows/first dance intact — they are the product
4. End on a wide, hold 2 s, fade audio not video

## Editing

- no aggressive silence removal; let moments breathe
- keep ambient audio under music; duck music 1.5 dB under speech
- colour consistency across cameras (match brightness, not a heavy LUT)
- no snap zooms, no whooshes; this is not a reel

## Gates

no shot shorter than 1.5 s · music never clips · speech audible · contact sheet
reviewed with the user · loudness ±1 LU
