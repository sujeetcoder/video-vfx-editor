# Playbook: clips from a lecture / webinar

**Use when** the source is teaching content (class, workshop, talk).

## Spec

- 1080×1920, 30 fps, 20–90 s clips, loudness −14 LUFS
- layout `fill` on the speaker, or `split` with slides below
- captions `clean`, slightly smaller, higher contrast

## Selection

- one complete idea per clip: a definition, a worked example, a correction
- prefer windows that open with the claim and close with the consequence
- reject clips that reference "as I said earlier" without context
- 4 clips is the default; 3 minimum for a useful batch

## Editing

1. keep the example intact — do not tighten to the point of skipping steps
2. reframe on the speaker; keep the slide visible in `split` when it carries data
3. captions must include technical terms correctly (check the transcript)
4. add a small title card (2 s) naming the concept

## Gates

complete idea · clean sentence start/end · slide readable in split ·
loudness ±1 LU · captions accurate · no claim changed by the cut
