# Playbook: vlog

**Use when** the source is a day-in-the-life, travel or behind-the-scenes.

## Spec

- 1080×1920 for social, 1920×1080 for YouTube; 30 fps
- loudness −14 LUFS, captions `bold-pop` on vertical
- duration 60–180 s for social, longer for YouTube

## Structure

1. Hook: the most interesting 2 s of the day, not "hey guys"
2. Loose chronology, but cut every location change with a hard cut
3. Keep the personality; remove only dead air and logistics
4. End on a plan, a view, or a line — not a wave goodbye

## Editing

- silence removal natural (0.7 s), keep laughs and reactions
- b-roll from `stock.py` only when the user has none; credits required
- captions with the speaker's actual words; never invent jokes
- music bed low (−20 dB) or none

## Gates

hook ≤1.5 s · no mid-sentence cuts · captions accurate to the transcript ·
loudness ±1 LU · b-roll credits present
