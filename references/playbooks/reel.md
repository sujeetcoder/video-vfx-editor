# Playbook: reel / short

**Use when** the deliverable is a vertical short up to 60 s.

## Spec

- 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264, AAC 192k, faststart
- loudness −14 LUFS, true peak −1 dBTP
- duration target 20–45 s, hard max 60 s

## Structure

1. Hook in the first 1.5 s (strongest line, no intro)
2. 3–6 beats, each 2–5 s, hard cuts on the beat or on a word
3. One idea only; cut anything that needs context
4. End on the payoff line, then 0.3 s of clean tail

## Editing

- silence removal tight (`min_silence 0.45`), fillers removed
- punch-in zoom ≤1.25× on emphasis words only
- no transitions except the cut; a 30 ms audio fade hides the join
- captions `bold-pop`, bottom 18 % safe zone, ≤32 chars

## Gates

hook ≤1.5 s · no mid-sentence start/end · caption not cut at the tail ·
subject inside the crop · loudness ±1 LU · contact sheet looked at
