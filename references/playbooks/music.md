# Playbook: music / beat cut

**Use when** cutting to a track (performance video, lyrics, montage).

## Spec

- 1080×1920 or 1920×1080, 30 fps (or 24 for cinematic)
- cuts on the beat grid, loudness −14 LUFS
- captions only for lyrics, `karaoke`

## Structure

1. Establish the artist/space in the first 2 s
2. Cut every 1–2 beats in the chorus, 2–4 in verses
3. One visual idea per section; repeat it for recognisability
4. End exactly on the last beat, no trailing black

## Editing

- `music` playbook uses `[0.6, 2.0]` shot lengths
- never time-stretch the song to fit the footage; cut footage to the song
- no dialogue ducking — the track is the star
- do not copy a reference track; the user must own/license the music

## Gates

cuts land on beats (within 80 ms) · no shot >4 s in the chorus · audio never
clips · loudness ±1 LU · lyrics match the words if burned
