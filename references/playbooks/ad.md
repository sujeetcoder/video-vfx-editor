# Playbook: ad / promo

**Use when** the deliverable sells something in under 45 s.

## Spec

- 1080×1920 (or 1:1), 30 fps, loudness −14 LUFS
- hard max 45 s; 15 s cut-down is a common second export

## Structure

1. Hook (problem or bold claim) in 0–2 s
2. Product on screen by 3 s — never hide it
3. 2–3 benefit beats, each ≤3 s
4. CTA card for 2–3 s, readable without sound
5. End on the product/logo, not on a fade to black

## Editing

- tight cut, punches on the beat, background music bed at −18 dB with ducking
- captions `bold-pop`, 2–3 words per line, never covering the product
- lower-third only for price/offer, not for narration
- do not add fake testimonials or invented claims — ask the user for copy

## Gates

hook ≤1.5 s · product visible by 3 s · CTA present · captions inside safe zone ·
loudness ±1 LU · no text over the platform UI strip
