# Playbook: tutorial

**Use when** the video teaches something: screen demo, coding, cooking, how-to.

## Spec

- 1920×1080, 30 fps, loudness −16 LUFS
- captions `clean` (readability over style)
- duration follows the content; do not pad

## Structure

1. One-sentence promise in the first 5 s
2. Steps in order, each step introduced by a short caption or chapter card
3. Screen content stays sharp: no reframe crop that cuts UI
4. End with a recap card (3 bullets max)

## Editing

- keep pauses around complex steps (do not tighten to the point of confusion)
- zoom into the UI region instead of cropping the whole frame
- highlight the cursor/region with a drawn box when it matters
- remove filler and false starts, keep explanatory repetition

## Gates

no text overflow in captions · screen text legible after scaling ·
step order preserved · loudness ±1 LU
